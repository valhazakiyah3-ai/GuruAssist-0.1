# PAI first implementation guide

This guide supports the first subject-specific example in GuruAssist. It is not a religious authority and does not replace the teacher, school, curriculum, or qualified scholars.

## PAI planning considerations

Depending on the curriculum and grade, PAI and Budi Pekerti may include Qur'an and Hadith, faith/aqidah, akhlak, fiqh/worship, and Islamic history. Confirm the actual scope from the applicable curriculum and school materials rather than assuming every topic belongs in every grade.

## Source-list discipline

Read `references/pai-approved-sources.md` for the initial project allowlist. The project owner selected that list for their own PAI configuration; other users may replace it with their own approved list.


- Ask which textbooks, school-approved materials, or reference list governs the lesson when this is not clear.
- If the teacher/user supplies a closed list of permitted books or sources, use **only** those sources for substantive religious explanations. Do not silently add internet sources, other books, or unsourced claims.
- Keep curriculum outcomes and school administrative requirements separate from religious evidence. Use the official curriculum/source document for curriculum wording and the teacher's permitted religious references for religious explanation.
- When no source text is supplied and no permitted source can be consulted, leave the relevant section as `[PERLU SUMBER/VERIFIKASI GURU]` rather than filling it from memory.

## Content verification requirements

- Do not fabricate Qur'anic Arabic, verse references, translations, hadith wording, narrators, authenticity grades, or citations.
- Use the teacher's approved textbook, official curriculum, or supplied references as the primary source.
- If a religious claim cannot be verified, mark it `[PERLU VERIFIKASI GURU]` and do not present it as settled fact.
- Distinguish between a quoted source, a paraphrase, and an AI-generated explanation.
- Where fiqh differences are relevant, avoid presenting one position as the only position unless the curriculum or teacher specifies the school of thought or approved reference.
- Avoid asking students to disclose private religious practice or family information.

## Pedagogical considerations

- Align knowledge, attitudes, and practical skills with the learning outcome and the actual lesson objective.
- Do not assume every PAI objective can be measured by a multiple-choice test.
- For recitation, worship practice, or demonstrations, use teacher observation and clear criteria where appropriate.
- Respect students' dignity and avoid shaming learners during practice or assessment.
- Technology can support access, demonstration, reflection, and feedback; it does not replace teacher modelling, guidance, or appropriate practice.

## Worked example boundary

The sample topic "salat berjamaah" in the repository is illustrative. Verify the grade placement, curriculum outcome, time allocation, fiqh details, and references against the teacher's current official materials before classroom use.
